Growth ≠ DevOps SaaS hockey stick · Integrations marketplace · ad-bought founders.

## 4. Fulcrum hitch (Ship-shaped, weak)

Ship rarely owns walkers. Prefer:

| Fulcrum | Hitch | Caution |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Operator’s own multi-repo habit | Dogfood → proof | Self only |
| Friend/founder with a stuck deploy | H1 once | Don’t become their DevOps employee |
| Agent/MCP users already shipping | Assist ritual | No fake completion |
| Obscur release adjacency | Honesty signal | P2 — not commercial spine |

Do not invent “Ship partner reseller network” as strategy.
