```text
Sell a cleared job the buyer can see before pay — not features, not “OSS + maybe help.”

```

## 1. What the template model actually sold

| Layer | SaaS templates (~2023–24 peak) |
|-------|--------------------------------|
| **Pain** | “I waste weeks on auth/Stripe/email/SEO before my idea” |
| **Proof** | Landing + screenshots/video of *already-wired* app; Discord social proof |
| **Deliverable** | Paid zip/repo — **local rights** on purchase (not hosted rental) |
| **Wall sentence** | Launch a SaaS in days, not months |
| **Price** | ~$149–$299 one-time (avoided weeks of setup) |
| **Not sold** | Infinite OSS · $29 “whole platform” · vague consulting |

That is **Model D-adjacent** in [PEER-BUSINESS-MODELS](./PEER-BUSINESS-MODELS-2026.md): a **digital product with a job wall**, priced like labor saved — not like a tip jar.

## 2. 2026 honesty (do not cosplay 2024)

| Fact | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| Generic Next.js auth+Stripe kits are **crowded**; AI compresses “I could wire this myself” | New generic boilerplate is a weak bet |
| Category leaders still sell, but revenue **down sharply** from peak (ShipFast-class public numbers) | Wall must be **narrower / harder to vibe-code** than “auth + Stripe” |
| Buyers still pay for **certainty of an architecture** and a **shown outcome** | Demo + specific pain beat feature matrices |
| Your $29 Solo failed as cold mid SKU | Template pricing band ($149–$299) matches avoided-pain math better **if** wall is real |

## 3. Operating grammar (what to sell)

```text
1. Name one pain in the buyer’s words (one sentence).
2. Show the solution in under 2 minutes (your recording / live URL / before-after).
3. Deliver a finite artifact they keep (repo · configured project · live URL standup).
4. Price against time/agency avoided — not against “software should be $29.”
```

| Good SKU shape | Bad SKU shape |
|----------------|---------------|
| “Medusa admin live on your VPS in one scoped pack” | “Orbit Yard Pro unlocks everything” |
| “Privacy analytics on your host — compose + proof URL” | “Integrations guide PDF” |
| “SaaS launch kit for *X* niche (underserved stack)” | “Yet another Next.js starter” |
| Paid local rights + optional paid standup | Infinite Gift + abstract H1 |

## 4. How to identify pains worth a SKU

Score a candidate **only if** all pass:

| # | Test | Fail if |
|---|------|---------|
| P1 | Buyer already spends money nearby (VPS, freelancer, Shopify apps) | Hobby-only, no budget |
| P2 | Outcome is **observable in a demo** (URL, admin, checkout test) | “Cleaner DX” vibes |
| P3 | Clone/AI cannot erase the wall in an afternoon | Pure auth+Stripe redux |
| P4 | You can deliver the artifact without becoming their DevOps forever | Open-ended retainer as day-one SKU |
| P5 | You have **or will build** a non-GitHub path to those buyers | “Put on GitHub and wait” |

## 5. Orbit’s role (if any)

| Role | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Delivery engine** | Sequence Get-live for the kit you sold |
| **Not the SKU** | Do not sell “the sequencer” as the template-era product |
| **Demo surface** | Record Soft-L3 of *the kit’s* wall, not abstract Publish chrome |

Orbit stays a means. The **monetizable product** is the **named outcome pack** (code + path + proof), priced template-class — or labor standup of that same wall.
