## Founder job (not the “Ops” label)

Entrepreneurs often hire freelancers/agencies for: deploy to a host, wire env/integrations, code signing, publish to stores/platforms, and assorted launch chores. They do **not** shop for “an Ops tool.” They want something **effortless, effective, low learning curve, done** — the same reason they outsource.

| Positioning trap | Honest path |
|------------------|-------------|
| Market as DevOps / CI competitor vs GitHub Actions · Vercel · Fastlane | Fail as a *category* (see [commercial-product-failed](./commercial-product-failed-design.md)) |
| Hide from all competition | Guarantees obscurity |
| Compete on **founder final-mile certainty** (URL · sign · publish gates) with less theater than agencies/pro Ops UIs | Open — this polish band |

Competition is not automatic failure. **Category** mimicry of professional Ops suites is. UX “professionalism” here means **easy answers to the launch job**, not denser control panels.

General surface language must sound like the hire (“get it live on Cloudflare”) not the toolkit (“hostdeploy · env import · portal”).
